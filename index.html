Help & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about doctor appointments, emergency protocols, insurance claims, and lab reports.

You can book an appointment directly through our online booking system on this website. Simply pick your preferred department or doctor, select an available date and time slot, and fill in your contact information. You will immediately receive a digital appointment pass with your token number, which can be printed or presented on your phone.
In an emergency, immediately call our 24/7 Trauma Hotline at 1-800-LIFE-CARE / (800) 543-3227 or submit your address through our Emergency Dispatch form. Our GPS-equipped mobile ICU ambulance will be dispatched to your location within minutes with emergency paramedics.
LifeCare Hospital is partnered with more than 45 premier health insurance carriers and Third-Party Administrators (TPAs) including BlueCross, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Medicare, and regional policies. Our 24/7 Insurance Desk handles cashless pre-authorizations directly with the insurer.
Visit the "Lab Reports" section on our website and enter your Patient ID or Lab Order Number (found at the top of your test receipt). You can view biological parameters, reference ranges, pathologist impressions, and download digitally signed PDFs.
General Inpatient visiting hours are 10:00 AM – 01:00 PM in the morning and 05:00 PM – 07:30 PM in the evening. ICU visiting hours are strictly limited to one immediate family member between 11:30 AM – 12:30 PM and 05:30 PM – 06:30 PM to maintain sterile patient environments.
